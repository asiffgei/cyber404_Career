# 🛡️ Cyber404 Career Finder

### Find your path in cybersecurity — one quiz at a time. 🚀

A fun, fast, completely free quiz that helps beginners discover which cybersecurity career track fits them best. Answer 15 quick questions, get a personalized match, and download your result to keep or share. 🎉

🔗 **[Try it live →](https://USERNAME.github.io/cyber404-career-finder/)**

---

## ✨ What it does

- 🧠 **15 quick, beginner-friendly questions** — no confusing jargon, just real scenarios
- 🎯 **Matches you to 1 of 5 career tracks**, ranked by how strongly your answers lean toward each
- 📊 **A full results breakdown** — your top match, your scores across all 5 tracks, and *why* it matched you
- 🗺️ **A suggested beginner roadmap** to start learning your matched track
- 📥 **Download your result as an image** — generated right in your browser, nothing uploaded anywhere
- 🎊 **A little celebration** when your results appear
- 📱 **Works great on phone, tablet, or desktop**
- 🔒 **100% private** — no sign-up, no tracking, no data ever leaves your browser

---

## 🧭 The 5 career tracks

| | Track | What it's about |
|---|---|---|
| 🛡️ | **Blue Team / SOC** | Monitoring systems, catching threats, defending organizations |
| 🎯 | **Red Team / Offensive Security** | Ethical hacking — finding weaknesses before attackers do |
| 🕵️ | **Digital Forensics & Incident Response** | Investigating exactly what happened after an attack |
| ☁️ | **Application & Cloud Security** | Securing apps, APIs, and cloud infrastructure |
| 📋 | **GRC & Security Management** | Risk, policy, compliance, and governance |
